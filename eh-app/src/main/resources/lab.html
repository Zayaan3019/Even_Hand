Even Hand Simulation Lab

A synthetic IIT Madras course, analysed by the real engine. In a real course the truth is hidden. Here we simulate it, so you can see how close the tool gets: every script is marked twice with the same luck, once by its own TA and once by a perfectly average one.

1Who marked what

Each column is a student (in roll-number order), each row a question; the colour is the TA whose mark counted. The audit reads only this picture, never a mark, and decides whether TA severity can be measured and whether it could have moved anyone.

2How differently did each TA mark? Truth vs. Even Hand

Percentage points of each question's maximum: +5 means half a mark less on a 10-mark question. Right of zero is harsher than the panel average.
