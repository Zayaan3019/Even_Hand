Write each question's maximum in brackets in its header. Columns such as Name or Total are ignored. A blank cell or AB means not answered. Several exams? Add an assessment column.

One row per answer (when TAs split by question)

roll_noassessmentquestionmax_marksmarksta

Column names are matched loosely: Roll No, roll-no and roll_no all work, and so do TA, marker or evaluator. A script marked twice is welcome: it is what links two TAs.
